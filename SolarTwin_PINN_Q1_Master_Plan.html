The Core Scientific Contribution (In One Paragraph):
When a Solar-Electric Vehicle (SEV) drives through urban streets (like Dhaka), roadside trees, buildings, and overhead structures cast rapidly moving partial shadows across the solar roof. Shaded substrings activate Schottky bypass diodes, fracturing the $P\text{--}V$ curve into multiple Local Maximum Power Points (LMPPs) and one Global Maximum Power Point (GMPP). Classical trackers (P&O, INC) get trapped in local peaks (losing 15%–42% power), while metaheuristics (PSO, GWO) require 40–110 iterative PWM sweeps ($0.4\text{--}1.5\text{ s}$), which is too slow for a moving vehicle. SolarTwin-PINN takes only 5 ultra-fast sparse voltage–current probes ($<8\text{ ms}$) and runs an INT8 Physics-Informed Neural Network (`2.14 ms`, `14.8 KB` Flash) embedded with Bishop bypass-diode inflection priors on an ESP32-S3 to jump straight to the exact Global MPP ($99.45\%$ efficiency), while a 3D Web Digital Twin tracks Li-ion Battery State-of-Health (SoH) and performs cloud recalibration.
